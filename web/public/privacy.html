Privacy

You can explore public facility records without creating an account. This page explains the information used when you visit or choose to sign in.

Last updated November 2, 2026

Information we use

When you create an operator account using email, Amazon Cognito stores your email address, account identifier, verification status, and authentication information. Cognito manages your password; WattDocket does not receive your password in its web app. When available, you may choose a social provider to sign in; that provider and Cognito exchange the identifiers and profile information needed to authenticate you, such as your email address and name if supplied.

Public facility cards are built from public records and reference estimates. An account does not verify facility ownership, and the current operator view uses public data.

When you use the site

Your browser requests map tiles from OpenStreetMap. If you use the address lookup, the address you enter is sent to the US Census Bureau's geocoding service. Font files are requested from Google Fonts. These services receive information necessary to respond, such as your network address and request details, under their own policies.

If you request a plain-English explanation, the service sends facts from the selected public facility snapshot to the model provider to produce an explanation. If the model cannot return a usable response, the site displays a labeled template instead.

Storage and service providers

The site uses browser session storage for sign-in state and your current session. Cognito and a social identity provider you select may also use cookies or related technologies for authentication. AWS provides hosting, delivery, authentication and the explanation service infrastructure; its systems may process technical request data for operation and security.

Retention and deletion

Account information is kept in Cognito while the account exists. You can request deletion of your WattDocket account and its associated account information by following our account deletion instructions. A provider may retain records under its own policies, or where needed for security, audit or legal obligations.

Contact

For privacy or account questions, email contact@wattdocket.com. Please do not send a password or verification code.
