WattDocket / Account support

Request account deletion

You can request deletion of your WattDocket operator account and associated account information.

Last updated November 2, 2026

How to request deletion

  1. Email contact@wattdocket.com with the subject “WattDocket account deletion.”
  2. Include the email address associated with the account and the sign-in method you used. Do not send your password or verification code.
  3. We will contact you if we need to verify the request before removing the account.

What this covers

Deletion removes your WattDocket account from its Amazon Cognito user pool. It does not delete your Google or Facebook account, public environmental records, or information retained separately by a provider under its own policies.
