Terms of use

WattDocket helps people explore public facility records and the assumptions behind reference estimates.

Last updated November 2, 2026

Records and estimates

Information is provided for general research and exploration. Public records can be incomplete or out of date, and reference estimates are not measurements of a facility's actual operations. Read the sources, dates and assumptions shown with each card before relying on a figure. WattDocket does not provide legal, regulatory, engineering or investment advice.

Accounts and acceptable use

Public pages can be used without an account. An operator account gives access to a view of public records; it does not establish ownership of a facility or make those records private. Keep your sign-in information secure, and do not interfere with the site, attempt unauthorized access, or abuse its services.

Availability and external services

The site and its data are provided as available and may change or be interrupted. The site links to external sources and uses third-party services; their content and availability are outside WattDocket's control.

Contact

Questions can be sent to contact@wattdocket.com. See the privacy page for information about accounts and the deletion page for account removal requests.
