# Code of conduct

WattDocket welcomes people with different backgrounds, experience levels, and perspectives. This policy applies to the project's issues, pull requests, reviews, and other project spaces.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and specific. Critique a claim, change, or method rather than the person presenting it.
- Make room for questions and disagreement. Explain evidence and acknowledge uncertainty.
- Respect privacy. Do not publish another person's private information or contact details without permission.
- Keep discussion relevant to the project and give credit for others' work.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, sustained personal attacks, and deliberate disruption are not acceptable.

## Reporting and response

Use GitHub's [reporting tools](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam) to report abusive content or behavior privately to GitHub. For a project concern that can safely be discussed in public, open an issue and mention the maintainer, [@pankajarm](https://github.com/pankajarm). Do not include sensitive details in public reports. If you already have a private contact channel with the maintainer, you may use it for a confidential project report.

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content, close or lock discussions, restrict participation, or block a participant. They will consider the context and severity of the conduct and may act immediately to protect participants. Retaliation against someone who raises a concern is itself a violation.
